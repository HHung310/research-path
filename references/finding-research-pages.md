# Finding research pages

Use these tips to find where a student's school lists professors and research. Always start at
the student's own school, and only use public pages.

## Where to look

1. **Department faculty directory.** Search "[school] [department] faculty research interests".
   Most departments list each professor with a short research summary and a link to their page.
2. **Lab and group pages.** Search "[school] [topic] lab" or "[school] [topic] research group".
   Lab pages often list current members, recent papers, and sometimes whether they take
   undergraduates.
3. **Undergraduate research office.** Search "[school] undergraduate research". Many schools
   have a page or program that lists openings, application dates and how to get started.
4. **Professor's own page.** Check for a "Join us", "Prospective students" or "Openings" section.
   Quote only what it says.
5. **Papers.** For recent work, search the professor's name with "Google Scholar" or the
   department's publications page, and read the titles and abstracts.

## What counts as "open to students"

Say it only when a page states it, such as "we welcome undergraduate researchers" or a listed
opening with a date. A professor who has a lab but no statement is "not stated". Never guess.

## If a search is thin

- Try the department name instead of the topic, or a related topic.
- Try the school's research centers or institutes.
- If nothing at the school fits, say so and offer matches from outside the school, labelled
  clearly as outside.
